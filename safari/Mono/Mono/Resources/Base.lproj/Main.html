One step left: turn Mono on in Safari.

  1. Open Safari Settings, choose Extensions and tick Mono.
  2. Open x.com, click the Mono button in the toolbar and choose Always Allow on This Website.

Mono is on.

Open x.com in Safari. The line at the bottom of the page switches looks, and ⌥⇧M turns Mono off and on.

This window closes when Settings opens.
